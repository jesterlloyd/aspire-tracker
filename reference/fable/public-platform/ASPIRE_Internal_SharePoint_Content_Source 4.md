# ASPIRE at a Glance

## What Is ASPIRE?

The Affiliate Students' Pathway from Internship to Residency Experience, or ASPIRE, is Cedars-Sinai's pathway for senior nursing students, including current employees, to complete hands-on bedside clinical rotations in specialized units while paired with experienced preceptors.

ASPIRE is designed to:

- build clinical confidence
- sharpen bedside skills
- support the transition into professional nursing practice
- strengthen preparation for the New-Graduate RN Residency Program

## Why Does Cedars-Sinai Offer ASPIRE?

Academic-practice partnerships strengthen nursing pipelines.

Structured student-to-practice transitions may improve:

- confidence
- competence
- readiness for practice
- workforce retention

# Eligibility

## Eligibility Checklist

Applicants should meet all applicable criteria:

- Be in the final semester of an accredited nursing program affiliated with Cedars-Sinai
- Be enrolled in an eligible program, including:
  - Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  - Accelerated Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  - Licensed Vocational Nurse to Bachelor of Science in Nursing
  - Master's Entry Clinical Nurse
  - Entry-Level Master's in Nursing
- Complete a hands-on bedside clinical rotation
- Have a cumulative GPA of 3.0 or higher on a 4.0 scale
- Complete a minimum of 90 hours in a direct patient-care role under preceptor supervision
- Meet educational, health, safety, and background requirements established by both Cedars-Sinai and the nursing school

## Important Eligibility Limitation

Leadership rotations and observational-only rotations do not meet ASPIRE criteria.

The rotation must include direct patient care.

# How to Apply

## Step 1: Notify the School Placement Coordinator

Students should inform their school's placement coordinator that they are interested in completing a senior rotation at Cedars-Sinai through ASPIRE.

The placement coordinator helps confirm:

- school affiliation
- final-semester status
- rotation requirements
- program eligibility
- school-specific prerequisites
- Cedars-Sinai requirements

## Step 2: Complete the Intake Form

After the school confirms eligibility, the student receives an intake form from a Nursing Professional Development Practitioner.

The form collects information such as:

- unit preferences
- specialty interests
- career goals
- learning goals
- scheduling information

A school placement coordinator may complete or support completion of the intake form when appropriate.

## Step 3: Participate in a Brief Interview

The ASPIRE team conducts a brief conversation to discuss:

- unit preferences
- specialty interests
- career goals
- learning objectives
- placement fit
- unit availability

Placement decisions consider both the student's interests and organizational workforce needs.

# Frequently Asked Questions

## Q1. Can I complete my senior rotation at Cedars-Sinai if I work here in a non-nursing role?

Yes, if all eligibility criteria are met.

The employee must:

- be in the final semester of an affiliated nursing program
- maintain a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0
- complete a hands-on bedside rotation
- satisfy all school and Cedars-Sinai requirements

The employee's current job classification does not determine eligibility.

## Q2. My school requires a leadership or observational rotation. Can I still participate in ASPIRE?

No.

ASPIRE requires a hands-on bedside clinical rotation involving direct patient care.

Students completing leadership or observational rotations may still explore other New-Graduate RN Residency pathways after graduation if they meet those pathways' requirements.

> **Public-use warning:** Verify current pathway names before publication.

## Q3. What happens if I do not pass the NCLEX immediately?

An RN license is required before beginning the New-Graduate RN Residency Program.

Students should confirm current timing and licensure requirements with the NGRP team.

## Q4. Can current Cedars-Sinai employees participate in ASPIRE?

Yes.

Current employees who meet ASPIRE eligibility criteria may apply.

## Q5. Can I apply to the New-Graduate RN Residency Program if I completed my senior rotation elsewhere?

Yes.

ASPIRE is one pathway into the New-Graduate RN Residency Program, but it is not the only pathway.

Applicants who meet the requirements of other residency pathways may still apply.

## Q6. How is unit placement determined?

Placement is based on:

- clinical interests
- career goals
- learning objectives
- unit availability
- preceptor availability
- workforce needs

The ASPIRE interview helps clarify preferences and potential placement options.

## Q7. Will I have the same preceptor throughout the rotation?

Continuity is the preferred model.

Students are generally assigned one primary preceptor to support:

- relationship development
- consistency
- progressive learning
- reliable feedback

In some circumstances, staffing or scheduling needs may require temporary or secondary preceptor support. These changes are coordinated with Nursing Professional Development.
