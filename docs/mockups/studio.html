ASPIRE Connect: Studio

A fifth Connect tab for newsletters, announcements and sign-ups that look designed, not typed. You tell Keith what you want to send; he asks what he needs, drafts the words, lays out the blocks, and proposes who should get it. You restyle anything, approve the list, and send through the same engine Outreach uses, with the same email rules, demo guard and Sent History. The worked example runs through every frame.

Connect gains Studio beside Outreach. The page opens on one question, "What do you want to send?", with a few starters from this cycle's phase. Below: starting layouts, your drafts, and what went out recently with its results.

Email-safe by construction. A design is stored as blocks (JSON), never as hand-written HTML. On send it is rendered to the table-based, inline-styled HTML that Outlook, Gmail and iPhone Mail all draw the same way, with a plain-text copy. Webfonts fall back to Georgia and Arial; colours are checked for contrast as you pick them.

A capacity cap turns later yeses into a waitlist. Anything that needs real answers uses the Catalog form block instead.
