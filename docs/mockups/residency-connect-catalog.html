Residency Portal: Connect and Catalog

Talent Acquisition gets the staff app's two communication apps in its own portal. ASPIRE Connect (header icon) holds Contacts, Messages and Outreach. Catalog (its own header icon, beside Connect) is the staff Catalog, ported. Everything below is the real component, drawn from the portal's side: same chrome as today's Residency Portal, same picker, same composer, same Catalog list.

1 · Connect › Messages, starting a private message

The New Message drawer gains a To choice. "ASPIRE Team" is today's shared team inbox. "A unit leader" and "An alumnus" start a private conversation between HR and that one person: the ASPIRE team's staff inbox never lists it. Only people with an active portal account appear, because Messages lives in their portal.

4 · Catalog (its own app, the header's second icon)

The staff Catalog in the portal's Modern drawing: categories on the left, the list, the detail panel. HR browses, opens, and sends. A file or link goes through Outreach; a form gets each person a personal link; a signature template goes out for signature. Adding, editing and deleting Catalog items stays with the ASPIRE team. Who received each form or signature request is tracked in the panel, as for staff.
