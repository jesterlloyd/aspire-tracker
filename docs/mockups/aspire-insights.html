ASPIRE Insights

Outcomes across every cohort, school, unit and pathway.

The portal's current At a Glance sections stay here, unchanged.

Definitions
Applicants
Everyone who submitted an ASPIRE application for the cohort.
Placed
Students matched to a unit and preceptor on the Placement Board.
Completed
Placed students who finished the rotation and the required clinical hours.
Applied to NGRP
Completed students who submitted a New Graduate RN Residency application. Denominator: completed students in finished cohorts.
Offered NGRP
Completed students who received an NGRP offer. Same denominator.
Hired into NGRP
Completed students who started NGRP. Same denominator. This is the headline rate.
Internal
Students who were Cedars-Sinai employees at the time of application. External is everyone else.
Unit yield
Students hired into NGRP on the same unit where they did their ASPIRE rotation, divided by students the unit hosted in finished cohorts.
Too few to rate
Any rate built on fewer than 5 students. The count still shows.
In rotation
A cohort still in its rotation counts toward Applicants and Placed, never toward outcome rates.
