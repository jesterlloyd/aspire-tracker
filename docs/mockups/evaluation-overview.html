What this is

A third Evaluation view, first in the picker: the surveys read the way ASPIRE Insights reads the pipeline. Responses (the packet and roster) and Review & Release do not change.

Reused, not invented

  • KPI cards: FilterKPICard, one chosen at a time.
  • Filter bar, chips and More filters: Insights' FilterBar.
  • Chart cards, Table view, CSV, hover: Insights' ChartCard.
  • Every figure: responsesPacketModel.js (bands, pairs, NA rules, the Likert rule). Nothing new is scored.
  • Ready to release: reviewQueueBuild.js, the queue Needs you reads.
  • Themes: the existing Keith comment themes.
  • Keith's Read: the Insights pattern, a second Skill.

Rules it keeps

  • A group under 5 responses is withheld, never zero.
  • Casey-Fink items are licensed: sections and means only, never item text.
  • Preceptor rating 1 (Not Observed) is left out of every mean and count.
  • Down is amber and textured, never red.
  • Keith is sent totals and means only: no comment text, no names. He quotes; he never computes.
  • Every bar opens Responses, filtered to the students behind it.

Owner decides

  1. Overview first in the picker, or after Responses?
  2. Does Co-Lead see it? (Responses: Owner, Admin, Co-Lead.)
  3. By Unit names units with 5+ responses. Show the unit leaders' view of the same numbers later?
  4. Should Insights' Casey-Fink card link here, and leave Insights to the pipeline?
